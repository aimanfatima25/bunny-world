Kawaii Corner
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Kawaii Corner is a cozy mini-game app made by Aiman Fatima. This page explains what information the game uses, why, and how you can delete it. We don't show ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't use tracking or analytics tools.

Information kept on your phone

Your stars, hearts, chosen character and friends, stickers, mailbox, daily rewards, best scores and aquarium are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them. The game also keeps a random player ID on your device so the server can recognise your game.

Information sent to our game server

Some features need our game server (api.aimanfatima.com):

  • Top 10 scores: the name you play under, your score and the game.
  • Friends (only if you pick a username): your username, display name, chosen character picture and animal, stars, hearts, the short motto on your ID card, your friends list and friend requests, and when you were last online (so friends can see you're online).
  • Playing together: your score and level while a match with a friend is running. Matches aren't stored after they end.

Your username, character, stars and online status can be seen by other players who search for you or are your friends.

Saving your progress with Google (optional)

If you choose "Continue with Google" in Settings, Google tells us your Google account ID and email address, and we save a copy of your game progress on our server so you can get it back on a new phone. We never see your Google password. You can sign out at any time in Settings.

Camera (Flappy Bunny hand control)

Flappy Bunny can follow your hand with the front camera, only if you choose "Hand wave" and allow the camera. The pictures are looked at on your phone, frame by frame, to find your hand. They are never saved, recorded or sent anywhere. You can always play with taps instead.

Other services

  • Google Fonts: the game's font is loaded from Google, which receives your device's IP address like any website visit (Google's privacy policy).
  • Google Sign-In: only if you use it (see above).

Children

Kawaii Corner is meant for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Settings → Delete my account. This deletes your username, profile, friends, Top 10 scores and Google-saved progress from our server straight away.

Without the app: email aimanfatima111225@gmail.com with the subject "Kawaii Corner account deletion" and your username. We'll delete everything within 30 days.

We keep server data only while your account exists. Short backups made during maintenance are deleted within 30 days.

Security

All connections to our server use HTTPS. Only the developer can access the server.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
