## Full description
Welcome to Kawaii Corner, a cozy little world full of cute mini-games! 🎀

🍣 COOKING SHOPS
Run your own Sushi Shop, Ramen Shop, Cake & Sweets bakery and Pancake Shop. Cook rice and noodles, stack the toppings just like the recipe, and serve happy customers before they run out of patience.

🐰 FLAPPY BUNNY
Fly a fluffy bunny through the pillars. Tap to hop, or try something new: fly with your hand in front of the camera, or by tilting your phone!

🎣 CATCH THE FISH
Move your net through the sea and catch as many fish as you can. Koi are worth extra, but watch out for pufferfish, sharks and crocodiles!

🐟 MERGE THE FISH & MORE
Merge little fish into rarer ones, pop bubbles, and test your memory.

💖 COLLECT & DECORATE
Earn stars, unlock cute girls, guys, animals and sea friends, fill your aquarium and collect stickers.

👭 PLAY WITH FRIENDS
Pick your own username, add friends with your ID card or QR code, and challenge them to play together in real time.

📅 DAILY REWARDS
Come back every day for stars in the 15-day login event.

☁️ SAVE YOUR PROGRESS
Sign in with Google in Settings to keep your game safe, even on a new phone.

No ads. Just cozy, cute fun. ♥

Music: "Yummy Flavor" and "Caramel" by PupClub.
