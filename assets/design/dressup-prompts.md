# Dress Up: prompts for the picture sheets

Make the base doll first. Then attach it as the reference picture in every other prompt.
Rules for every sheet: pure white background, no shadows, no text or numbers, items not
touching each other, same soft pastel watercolor chibi style as Bunny World.

## 1. Base doll (one picture)

```
A single cute chibi girl doll for a dress-up game, in soft pastel watercolor kawaii style
with thin brown outlines. Full body, standing straight, facing the front, arms hanging
slightly away from the body, legs slightly apart, feet flat. She wears only a plain white
camisole and plain white shorts, barefoot. Short neat hair tied tightly back so her face,
ears and neck are fully visible. Big dark eyes, small mouth, soft pink cheeks. Centered on
a pure white background, no shadow, no text. Tall portrait image, the doll fills most of
the height.
```

## 2. Hair sheet (heads only)

```
Using the attached doll as the exact reference: 12 copies of her head and shoulders in a
4 by 3 grid on a pure white background. Every copy has exactly the same face, same size,
same position and same angle. Only the hairstyle changes: long wavy, long straight,
medium wavy, short bob, high ponytail, low ponytail, half up half down, messy bun, twin
tails, braids, long curly, curtain bangs. Hair goes down as far as it naturally would.
Soft pastel watercolor chibi style, thin brown outlines, no shadows, no text, nothing
touching.
```

(Make a second hair sheet for more styles or colors: blonde, black, pink, lilac.)

## 3. Tops sheet

```
Paper-doll clothing sheet made to fit the attached doll exactly. 12 tops, each shown
alone (no doll, no body), front view, flat, as if worn, sized for the doll's chest and
arms, sleeves pointing down like the doll's arms. Arranged in a 4 by 3 grid with space
around each one, on a pure white background. Tops: basic tee, graphic bunny tee, button
shirt, oversized striped shirt, cozy sweater, bunny hoodie, pink cardigan, crop top,
tank top, off-shoulder top, black turtleneck, ribbon blouse. Soft pastel watercolor chibi
style, thin brown outlines, no shadows, no text.
```

## 4. Bottoms sheet

```
Paper-doll clothing sheet made to fit the attached doll exactly. 12 bottoms, each shown
alone (no doll, no legs), front view, flat, as if worn, sized for the doll's waist and
legs. 4 by 3 grid with space around each one, pure white background. Bottoms: wide leg
jeans, straight jeans, baggy cargo pants, sweatpants, black leggings, pleated pink skirt,
white tennis skirt, denim skirt, A-line skirt, denim shorts, black shorts, pajama pants.
Soft pastel watercolor chibi style, thin brown outlines, no shadows, no text.
```

4 by 3 grid with space around each one, pure white background. Dresses: pink frilly

## 6. Shoes sheet

Paper-doll clothing sheet made to fit the attached doll exactly. 12 pairs of shoes, each

## Makeup

No sheet needed: blush, lip tint, eyeshadow, freckles and face sparkles are drawn on the
doll's face in the game.
